# Reachout Context

## Product Summary

Networking and informational interview tracker for job seekers.

## Vision

Help job seekers build meaningful professional connections through organized outreach, interview tracking, and relationship management.

## Operating Constraints

- Follow repository-native tooling and deployment conventions.
- Keep changes backward-compatible unless a migration is explicitly planned.
- Capture high-impact decisions in docs/specs before implementation.

## Core Commands

- Install: `npm install`
- Dev: `node api-server.js`
- Test: `npm test`
- Lint: `npx eslint .`
- Build: `No build step (runtime Node app)`
